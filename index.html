About

A creative practice grown from place, imagination, and connection.

Michelle St-Denis is a multidisciplinary artist working in digital art, painting, and community-based creative projects. Inspired by nature, storytelling, and cultural connections, she enjoys creating meaningful artwork that brings people together.

Michelle also facilitates painting workshops within her community and collaborates with other artists on creative initiatives. Alongside her artistic practice, she is passionate about UX design and is actively developing her skills in user experience and digital design.
